Common Questions

FAQ

What is NIBAD 2026?

NIBAD 2026 is the Nigerian Independence Ball, Award and Dinner, a premium recognition program celebrating Nigerian excellence and global impact.

How can I nominate someone?

Use the nomination form with nominee details, category, and a short achievement summary.

Who can be nominated?

Individuals, brands, organisations, creatives, leaders, innovators, and changemakers with outstanding contributions can be nominated.

When will nominations close?

Nominations close on September 15, 2026. Finalists will be announced on September 25, 2026.

How can I buy tickets?

Choose Regular, VIP, or VVIP access in the ticket section and complete your purchase securely on Evenzer. Sponsor tables are arranged directly with the NIBAD team.

Can brands sponsor the event?

Yes. Brands can request sponsor packages through the sponsor inquiry link.

What is the dress code?

Formal gala, black-tie, evening wear, or refined cultural attire is recommended.

Where will the event take place?

The event will take place at St. Gabriel Community Center.

Will there be media coverage?

Yes. Media moments, red carpet coverage, interviews, and event highlights are planned.
